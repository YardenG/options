Team Selection

Usecase

Define a common component for a team selector that serves the pages of deals and stats.

Requirements

Define a common component for a user/team selector that serves both pages of deals and stats.

The selector should enable all the relevant users to easily view their own data.

Make sure you take under consideration the fact that some users (such as VP sales) may have several users or teams reporting to them.

The solution

A Team selector dropdown that allows each user to view select and view only the relevant options according to his persona.

Account executives (AEs)

Account executives can select to view their own data or
to view their whole team's data (including their own).
